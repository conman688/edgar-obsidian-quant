## Checks

- Leak check (value, 3m excess): real 0.1%, leaky 1.9%. PASS: the harness rewards look-ahead, so it can detect it.
- Survivorship gap: 57.2% of active non-financial filers had no price on an average date (mostly later delisted). Results are an upper bound.
- Picks that stopped trading inside the 3m window (exit at last close): {'default': 0, 'drawdown_50': 0, 'loose': 0, 'value': 0, 'insider_fcf': 0}.
- Picks left out of the 3m figures because the price series broke (10x in a day): {'default': 0, 'drawdown_50': 0, 'loose': 0, 'value': 0, 'insider_fcf': 0}.
- Universe and picks: names with a close of at least $1 on the rebalance date.
