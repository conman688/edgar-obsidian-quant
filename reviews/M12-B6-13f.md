### What changed
- `src/ingestion/form13f.py`:
  - `deadline(period)`: the quarter end + 45 days, rolled past weekends. Presidents Day is the
    only federal holiday a rolled deadline can meet.
  - `window_dates(name)` and `home_period(start, end)`: each zip holds exactly one quarter's
    deadline.
  - `load_window`:
    - Refuses a filing dated outside its window.
    - Reports for the home quarter filed by the deadline:
      - Cover-page amendments (ISAMENDMENT Y; empty is N; or a 13F-HR/A).
      - Order key (filed, amendment no., accession).
      - The newest original or RESTATEMENT stands. NEW HOLDINGS after it add to it.
      - A typeless amendment is counted and left out.
    - Lines used: SH with no PUT/CALL, CUSIP upper-cased.
    - Dropped and counted: a line priced more than 0.5 decades off the CUSIP's median, unless
      it is that close to 1,000x off (value in thousands).
    - Writes `form13f_positions` (manager, CUSIP, shares) for the quarter, then
      `form13f_quarters`.
    - `_apply_changes` then sets `holders_new` and `holders_exited` for that quarter and the
      next. Each comes from the stored positions of the quarter and the one before only, among
      managers that reported in both.
    - Counted per quarter in `form13f_periods`: late reports, on-time reports for other quarters
      (`elsewhere`), bad CUSIPs (length or check digit) and outlier lines.
  - `parse_ftd` / `load_ftd`: `cusip_symbols` (CUSIP, symbol, first and last settlement date).
  - `load(con, client, work)`: FTD files first, then the zips in window order. Each file is
    loaded once (ledger) and deleted after loading. A failure is retried on the next run.
  - `Form13fClient` uses SecClient only: SEC hosts, project User-Agent.
- Migration 13: `form13f_quarters`, `form13f_periods`, `form13f_positions`, `cusip_symbols`.
- `src/db/queries.py`:
  - `cusip_periods_sql()`: under one symbol, CUSIPs take turns by first sighting. Each is
    valid until the next one's first sighting, or until its own last sighting + 92 days if
    that is earlier. The symbol must be a current ticker of exactly one CIK.
  - `institutional_sql()`: the newest quarter with on-time reports whose deadline is
    `<= as_of`. It reads the CUSIP valid at that quarter's end, and only if the quarter is
    inside the company's price history.
- `src/screen.py`: `informational_sql` only (after ranking). New columns:
  - `inst_period`, `inst_known_at`, `inst_holders`, `inst_holders_new`,

  - `inst_pct`: 13F shares on the price date's split basis, over cover-page shares.
  - `inst_pct_note`:
    - `adr` and `share_class`, from the title of class: no share is shown.
    - `untrusted_shares`: no share is shown.
    - `above_100pct`: the share is shown.
  - `metrics_sql` and `rank_sql` are unchanged.
- Vault: a company-note section, a dashboard "13F quarter" date, `Sources/SEC Form 13F data
  sets.md`, and frontmatter `inst_holders`, `inst_pct`, `inst_pct_note`.

### Where bugs are most likely
- `_apply_changes` order: loading quarter X recomputes X and X+1. A gap (X-1 absent) leaves
  `has_changes` false and the counts NULL.
- The outlier rule depends on the median price of the CUSIP's lines. With 1-2 holders the median
  is the bad line itself, so it is kept.
- `cusip_periods_sql`:
  - The first CUSIP under a symbol is valid back to year 1. Only the price-history test stops
    an old issuer from covering the new company's earlier quarters.
  - A ticker change (FB to META, same CUSIP) maps through the newest symbol only.
- `inst_pct` uses `split_factor` like A3. A bad split row in the price layer gives a bad share
  (CTVA).
- `home_period` for the old quarterly sets: a Q4 report is in the next year's q1 zip.

### Known gaps (not findings unless they are worse than stated)
- Managers sharing discretion, and parents and subsidiaries, can report the same shares. Above
  100% is shown and flagged, never corrected.
- Shorts, options, small and confidential positions are not in 13F. "No position reported" is
  never "not held".
- Late reports and amendments after the deadline are left out by design. A zip is loaded once:
  if SEC re-posts the sets again, nothing is refreshed.
- Mapped on the current ticker (like A3), so delisted companies are not mapped.
- Not scheduled. Nothing is loaded into the live DB, and migration 13 is not run there.
- Crowding, copy-trading, manager selection and weights stay out of scope (2026-11-17
  brainstorm).

### C1. Lines kept as "value in thousands" can come from a filing whose shares are wrong
- Severity: medium (wrong number)
- Where: src/ingestion/form13f.py:363-370 (the outlier rule judges each line alone)
- Evidence: the 1,000x band (±0.5 decade around 1e-3 of the median price) cannot tell

  and 4.9M shares in Q1 2026, then 507.8M shares at 2026-06-30). 19 of its 23 lines are 1e-4 to
  1e-6 of the median and are dropped. Four are kept:
  - GNTX 187.2M shares worth $7.7M (log -2.79);
  - HTFL 77.3M (-2.95);
  - NVO 129.9M (-3.33);
  - 579780206 at -3.38.

  - GNTX 13F shares are 392.5M against 205.3M outstanding, so the screen at 2026-11-20 shows

  shares. One more line is kept only because it is its CUSIP's median: XXEC 565251080,
  104.7M shares at $0.008.
- Possible fix:
  - Judge the filing: when most of a filing's lines are outliers, drop the whole filing and
    count it.
  - Or read the 1,000x band as thousands only when the filing's own lines are mostly in that
    band.
  - Either way, the legitimate thousands filers stay. In this quarter they are 146,720 lines
